To whoever finds this 26 · V · 2026

Dear you both,

You're planning a wedding, which means a hundred small decisions have already asked something of you this week. So let me make this one easier. My name is Uriel, and I photograph weddings to hold the day still long enough that you can return to it years later and feel it again.

I shoot around eighteen weddings a year on purpose, leaving the room to listen to yours. If your day feels close to any of this, write back. I read every note myself.

Yours Uriel,

THE APPROACH

From first letter
to last frame.

№ 01

A first letter

You write to me about the two of you. Where you met, how the day looks in your head, what scares you about being on camera. I read every note myself, and I write back.

№ 02

A slow plan

We meet, over coffee in the city or a video call, and walk through your day hour by hour. I send a quiet, honest proposal. No upsells, no urgency, no panic.

№ 03

The day, kept

I arrive before the light shifts and stay until the last dance. You'll forget I'm there. A few weeks later, your photographs arrive, along with a printed album, if you chose one.
